MUSIC — SONGS WITH STORIES

Songs with stories behind them. Press play to listen.

You Exist

NEGA-ISSEO

I wrote "I wonder if I can call this love" in the lyrics and asked my dad. He said, "That IS love" — turning toward someone, like a sunflower to the sun. The song where he granted me the word "love."

I'm Coming

POEM SONG — THE RIVER EP2

The ending song of the animated series The River EP2. Walker's song to his daughter Nara — crossing the river, passing the Dome, a father finding his way home. They never meet on screen in EP2, but this song connects them.

First Listening

POEM SONG

A song about listening with the heart for the first time — not with ears, but with the chest. Connected to the River's truth: "Who hears the river, handles time."

Almost

POEM SONG · CELLO

A poem on a cello melody — the cello is dad's instrument. A song about a heart that almost reached someone — and how reaching itself was enough.
