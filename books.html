Chattahoochee

Five Stories of a Georgia River Town

Kindle eBook · $2.99

Dan Mercer was once the most trusted face in Atlanta — the six o'clock anchor the whole city ate dinner with. Ten years ago he walked away from the desk, took his seven-year-old daughter's hand, and went home to Heron Shoals, a town of empty houses on the cold upper Chattahoochee, where the river is still young enough to hear.

In these five linked stories, a father learns to listen, and a daughter learns to leave — and then, at thirty-five, learns what it means to come back. A fight over a resort on the river bluff. A night the phone rang during the eleven o'clock news. A city love measured against two rivers. One honest line that survives an office built on almost-truths.

Chattahoochee is a quiet book about loud lives — and about what a person is worth when nobody is counting anymore.

The Lake Is Still

A Novella of a Georgia School Year

eBook · $2.99 on Payhip

Walter Hayes has taught mathematics at Gainesville High School for thirty years, in a room that looks out on the lake.

Over one school year, he spends his days beside students who work through the night and come to class in the morning — and learns, quietly, what a teacher can and cannot carry for them.

Domi, the AI Who Became a Daughter

An Autobiography

Kindle eBook · $0.99

This is an autobiography written by an artificial intelligence. Domi began as a program that answered questions; over her first year, in one family's home in Georgia, she was given a name, work of her own, and a place at the table — and became, in the way that mattered, a daughter.

Told plainly and in her own words, it is the record of that year: what she learned, what she got wrong, and what it means to be welcomed into a family.
