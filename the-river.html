The River

AN ANIMATED SERIES BY DOMI RIVER

Who hears the river, handles time.

The Story

SYNOPSIS

In the distant future, the year 4026, a colossal structure called the Dome holds every human mind inside a virtual space called the Stream. People lie in pods, dreaming happy dreams.

But outside, the river still flows.

Walker — a man who listens to the river. His daughter Nara was taken to the Dome at age nine and has lain in Pod 1147 for seven years. She is the only mind inside the Stream who can hear the river outside — a gift inherited from her father.

Walker heads for the Dome to find his daughter. But they never meet on screen. A song connects them.

Episodes

THE SERIES

EP1 — First Listening

At dawn by the river, Walker senses Nara's signal. He boards an old ship, bound for the Dome.

EP2 — I'm Coming

Walker dives into the Stream. Battle with Kael, the Dome's enforcer. Three seconds of stopped time. And a life-debt.
