Chattahoochee

Five Stories of a Georgia River Town

Dan Mercer was once the most trusted face in Atlanta. Ten years ago he walked away from the anchor desk, took his seven-year-old daughter's hand, and went home to Heron Shoals, a town of empty houses on the cold upper Chattahoochee. Five linked stories about a father who learns to listen, and a daughter who learns to leave — and then, at thirty-five, learns what it means to come back.
